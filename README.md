# Dubai Twin

Dubai Twin is an AI-powered government service execution platform for Dubai and the UAE.

The platform helps individuals, residents, visitors, companies, HR teams, and PRO teams start, prepare, execute, track, and resume Dubai and UAE government service workflows across web, WhatsApp, and an AI Avatar experience.

Dubai Twin started as a trusted AI city assistant for Dubai information, transport, weather, events, and official sources. The new version keeps those trusted information services and expands into real government service execution workflows such as document readiness, company wallets, employee government records, payments, request tracking, and cross-channel continuity.

## About the Project

Dubai Twin is designed to become a smart digital layer for Dubai and UAE government services.

The platform combines conversational AI, verified service information, document processing, request tracking, payment workflows, and multi-channel interaction to help users manage government service journeys from one place.

Users can start a process on the website, continue through WhatsApp, upload documents, receive updates, and resume the same request later without starting again.

## What Dubai Twin Offers

### Government Service Execution

Dubai Twin helps users prepare, execute, and track government service workflows, including:

- Company setup support
- Trade license renewal preparation
- Employee residence and Emirates ID workflows
- Medical test and health insurance workflows
- Labour contract and work permit preparation
- DEWA payment support
- Salik payment support
- Dubai parking payment support
- Document readiness and verification
- Request tracking and follow-up

### Connected Channels

Dubai Twin is built for cross-channel service continuity:

- Start from the website
- Continue on WhatsApp
- Interact with the AI Avatar
- Upload documents from any supported channel
- Receive updates and resume later from the same request

- Weather and official source-based city information

## Vision

Dubai Twin aims to become one intelligent platform that brings Dubai and UAE government services, trusted city information, documents, payments, and request tracking into one connected experience.

The long-term vision is to help people in the UAE manage their daily life, company needs, employee services, and government-related processes from one trusted digital place.

## Mission

Dubai Twin’s mission is to simplify access to Dubai and UAE services by turning complex government journeys into guided, trackable, and user-friendly digital workflows.

## Official Source Focus

Dubai Twin is designed around trusted and official information sources, including government portals, transport services, weather platforms, event systems, and verified service references.

Dubai Twin is not a government entity. It is an independent AI assistant and service execution platform designed to help users understand, prepare, and manage service workflows using trusted information and structured digital assistance.
